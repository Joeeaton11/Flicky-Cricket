NOMINATION NAME
Flicky Cricket launch: one-swipe arcade cricket, with a Christmas Cup at launch

DESCRIPTION (what's new and why it matters)
Flicky Cricket is a brand-new arcade cricket game built for one thumb. You read the ball, time a single swipe and smash it into the crowd. It is quick to learn, hard to master and perfect for a two-minute break.

It launches on 12 December with more than 120 hand-painted grounds, from a village green to beaches, volcanoes, a neon city and the Moon, each with its own look, crowd and bounce of the ball. There are 27 leagues to climb, quick one-on-one duels, weekend knockout cups, bags of kit to open and a postcard for every ground you conquer.

A Christmas Cup event runs from launch day to 4 January with festive kit and a snowy look, so there is a reason to play through the holidays.

WHY NOW / WHY IT FITS APPLE
- Cricket has billions of fans and very few light, fun, one-handed games. Flicky Cricket is made for casual players, not just hardcore fans.
- Made by a small independent UK team led by a club captain and former professional cricketer.
- Plays offline, has no account sign-up, and supports iPhone and iPad, with Game Center leaderboards.
- Year-round live events follow the cricket calendar (a Spring T20 Carnival, a summer of Tests, a World Showdown in October), so there is always something new.
- Free to play; purchases are optional cosmetics and bundles, and there is a one-off No Ads purchase.
