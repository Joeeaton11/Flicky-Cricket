APP REVIEW NOTES (App Store Connect > App Review Information > Notes; Play Console > App access notes)

Flicky Cricket is a free arcade cricket game. No account or sign-in is needed: tap PLAY on the home screen to start a match, then swipe up on the screen as the ball arrives to bat.

In-app purchases (all optional) are in the SHOP tab at the bottom left:
- Gem packs (Gems & Coins tab)
- Starter Pack, League Packs, Gem Jar and Super Over offers (Deals tab; some appear only after certain matches)
- No Ads (a one-off, permanent purchase; it can be restored with Settings > Restore Purchases)
- Flicky Pass and Flicky Pass+ (Events tab > Pass): one-off purchases for the current season's rewards, not subscriptions
- Event Bundle (Events tab, during an event)

Bags pay out a random selection of rewards. The chance of each reward tier is shown before opening. Bags can be earned by playing; gems used to speed them up can also be bought.

Ads: Google AdMob, with Google's consent form for the UK and EU and Apple's tracking prompt on iOS. Players can change ad choices in Settings > Privacy Choices.

Game Center is used for leaderboards and to keep progress on a new phone; it is optional.

Contact: support@flickycricket.com
