APP NAME (30 max)
Flicky Cricket

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 max)
Swipe to smash huge sixes! One-thumb arcade cricket with leagues, cups and kit.

FULL DESCRIPTION
ONE SWIPE. HUGE SIXES!

Flicky Cricket is the free arcade cricket game you play with one thumb. Read the ball, time your swipe and send it sailing into the crowd.

SWIPE TO BAT
Short ball? Pull it. Full and straight? Drive it back over the bowler's head. Every shot comes from one swipe, so it's quick to learn and hard to master.

120+ GROUNDS, 27 LEAGUES
Work your way from the village green to floodlit stadiums, beaches, volcanoes and beyond. Every league is a new place to play and a new trophy to lift.

1V1 DUELS AND WEEKEND CUPS
Take on rivals in quick-fire duels, then chase the big prizes in cup weekends. The Christmas Cup is on from launch day.

OPEN BAGS, BUILD YOUR KIT
Win bags, pop them open and collect bats, pads, helmets and legendary kit. Build a kit that looks the part.

COLLECT POSTCARDS
Every ground you conquer sends a postcard home. How many can you collect?

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Free to play, with optional in-app purchases
• Short matches, perfect for a quick break
• Bright, cartoon cricket for everyone

Flicky Cricket is free to download and play. Some optional items can be bought with real money. You can turn off in-app purchases in your device settings.
