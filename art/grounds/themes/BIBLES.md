# Theme bibles — paste these words unchanged into every prompt for that theme

## Pirate Cove
- THEME: Pirate Cove — a tropical island cricket ground built by pirates: weathered wooden stands with red canvas awnings, a cheering pirate crowd, a pirate ship anchored in a turquoise bay, palm trees, barrels, treasure chests, lanterns, cannons, rope and brass details.
- LIGHT: warm late-afternoon sun from picture-left.
- Grass: lush tropical turf with broad mown stripes. Rope: thick tan ship's rope with small black skull-flag markers. Inner ring: small white shells.
- FIELDER: a cheerful pirate deckhand, big round head, short stocky body, red bandana with knot at the back, plain mid-blue short-sleeved shirt (one flat colour), dark brown knee-length trousers, bare brown sandals, tanned skin.
- BOWLER: a lanky pirate captain, black tricorn hat with a gold trim, long dark red coat with gold buttons, black boots, curly black beard.
- KEEPER: a broad pirate in a black bandana, plain mid-blue shirt, big brown leather gloves and brown leather pads.
- UMPIRE: an old grey-bearded ship's quartermaster, white wide-brimmed hat, cream shirt, navy waistcoat, brown trousers.
- Ball: dark red leather with a gold seam. Stumps: carved driftwood posts with brass caps. Pitch: worn ship's deck planks.
- Words: chunky gold pirate lettering with a dark brown rope-like outline.

## T20 Super League (Indian franchise night stadium)
- THEME: a packed modern Indian T20 franchise stadium on match night: tiered stands full of fans waving flags and phone lights, floodlight towers, giant screens, fireworks and confetti cannons, dancing cheer squad on a podium, dugouts, sponsor-free LED boards in purple and gold, a city skyline beyond.
- LIGHT: bright white floodlights from picture-right.
- Grass: deep green floodlit turf with crisp checkerboard mowing. Rope: purple and gold padded boundary cushion. Inner ring: small white dashes.
- FIELDER: a young franchise cricketer, big round head, short stocky body, plain mid-orange T20 shirt (one flat colour), navy trousers, white trainers, navy cap.
- BOWLER: a tall fast bowler in a plain mid-orange shirt, navy trousers, white trainers, a sweatband, headband.
- KEEPER: plain mid-orange shirt, navy helmet, white keeping gloves and white pads.
- UMPIRE: black shirt, grey trousers, wide black sun hat, sunglasses.
- Ball: white T20 ball with a black seam. Stumps: LED stumps that glow red. Pitch: dry golden-brown with sharp white creases.
- Words: bold glossy 3D lettering, gold with a purple outline and a sparkle.

## Gully Cup — 1a `gully` (Mumbai neighbourhood)
- THEME: the neighbourhood IS the stadium — pastel chawl tenements with packed balconies, washing lines, rooftop kids with kites and water tanks, chai stall, auto-rickshaws, scooters, fruit cart, marigolds, bunting, banyan tree; Mumbai skyline and sea beyond.
- LIGHT: hot afternoon sun from picture-right. Field: sun-baked red-brown earth. Boundary: low painted kerb.
- F1: skinny teen boy, orange sleeveless vest, blue shorts, flip-flops, messy black hair.
- F2: chubby boy, white school shirt, navy shorts, red cap worn backwards.
- F3: girl, long black plait, bright pink kurta top, white leggings, white sneakers.
- F4: moustached uncle, white vest, blue-and-white checked lungi tucked up at the knee, brown sandals.
- KEEPER: small kid in a yellow T-shirt with huge mismatched keeping gloves. BOWLER: lanky teen, purple T-shirt, red headband. UMPIRE: moustached auto-rickshaw driver in khaki uniform with sunglasses.
- Props: concrete strip pitch with chalk creases; taped wooden-stick stumps; tennis ball wrapped in red tape; boundary of painted old tyres; ring marker small orange brick.
- HUD: Mumbai truck-art — bright painted tin with flowers, mirrors and tassels. Words: truck-art hand lettering.
