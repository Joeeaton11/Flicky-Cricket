    About Flicky Cricket

    Flicky Cricket is a free arcade cricket game for iPhone and Android, launching on 12 December 2026. You bat with one thumb: watch the bowler run in, then swipe up as the ball arrives to play your shot. Time it right and it flies for four or six. Each match is a quick 12-ball chase, so a game takes about two minutes.

    Play your way round the world across more than 120 cricket grounds in 27 leagues, from a back-street gully and a village green to the beach, a harbour city, a pirate cove, the Arctic, a volcano and the moon. Collect and upgrade bats, gloves, helmets, pads and shoes that change how you play, and unlock legendary kit as you climb.

    Game modes

    • League matches Chase the target in 12 balls and climb through 27 leagues.
    • 1v1 duels You and your opponent face the same 12 balls from the same bowler. Most runs takes the pot.
    • Weekend cups 32-player knockouts every weekend. Lose and you're out.
    • Weekly tournaments A score attack on the leaderboard.
    • Seasonal events Themed cups with exclusive kit, starting with the Christmas Cup at launch.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Flicky Cricket free?

    Yes. Flicky Cricket is free to download and free to play. There are optional in-app purchases for coins, gems and kit, and a one-off No Ads purchase that removes the ads between matches.

    When is Flicky Cricket coming out?

    Flicky Cricket launches on iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play) on 12 December 2026, with the Christmas Cup event. You can already play it free in your web browser at flickycricket.com/play.

    Can I play Flicky Cricket in my browser?

    Yes. Go to flickycricket.com/play on your phone, tablet or computer to play straight away, with no download needed.

    How do you play Flicky Cricket?

    You bat with one thumb. Watch the bowler, then swipe up as the ball arrives to play your shot. Time it perfectly to hit fours and sixes, and chase down the target before your 12 balls run out.

    Can I play against my friends?

    Yes. In 1v1 duels you and your opponent face the same 12 balls from the same bowler, and whoever scores the most runs takes the pot.

    Do I need an account?

    No. You don't need to sign up or give an email address. Your progress is saved on your phone, and you can sign in to Game Center or Google Play Games to keep it safe on a new device.
