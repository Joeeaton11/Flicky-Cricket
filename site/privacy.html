Flicky Cricket Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Flicky Cricket ("the game") is published by Flicky Cricket ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

The short version

  • You don't create an account and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress (coins, gems, kit, league results) is saved on your device and, if you sign in to Game Center or Google Play Games, copied to that account.
  • We use a small number of trusted services to show ads, process in-app purchases and understand how the game is played. They are listed below.
  • You can say no to personalised ads and to tracking, and you can still play the whole game.

Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card or bank details.

This website

flickycricket.com counts visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and collects no personal information. It records things like the page visited, how far down the page people get and whether the Play button is tapped, along with the browser, screen size and country, so we can see what works. Your IP address is not stored.

Ads and your choices

If you are in the UK, the EU or the EEA, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. On iPhone, Apple also asks whether the game may track you. If you say no, you will still see ads, but they won't be personalised. You can change your mind at any time in your device settings (iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Buying "No Ads" removes interstitial ads.

Legal basis (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

We rely on your consent for personalised ads and tracking, on contract to deliver purchases, and on our legitimate interests for anonymous analytics that keep the game working and fair.

Children

Flicky Cricket is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has used the game in a way that shared information with us, contact us and we will help delete it.

How long information is kept

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to restore your purchases and meet tax law. Game progress stays on your device until you delete the game.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete information about you, to object to its use, or to withdraw consent. Email us at the address below. In the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
