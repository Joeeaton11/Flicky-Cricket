Short description

One swipe. Huge sixes. Flicky Cricket is a free arcade cricket game you play with one thumb: swipe to time your shot and smash it out of more than 120 grounds, from the beach to the moon.

Long description

Flicky Cricket is a free arcade cricket game for iPhone and Android. You bat with one thumb: watch the bowler run in, then swipe up as the ball arrives to play your shot. Each match is a quick 12-ball chase that takes about two minutes.

Players work their way through 27 leagues and more than 120 cricket grounds, from a back-street gully and a village green to a beach, a harbour city, a pirate cove, the Arctic, a volcano and the moon. They can take on other players in 1v1 duels where both face the same 12 balls, play 32-player knockout cups every weekend, and collect bats, gloves, helmets, pads and shoes that change how they play. Themed events with exclusive kit run through the year, starting with the Christmas Cup at launch.

Key features

  • One-thumb, one-swipe batting: easy to pick up, hard to master
  • 120+ grounds across 27 leagues, from the beach to the moon
  • 1v1 duels: same 12 balls, same bowler, most runs takes the pot
  • Weekend cups: 32-player knockouts
  • Collectable kit with real effects on your game
  • Seasonal events, starting with the Christmas Cup
