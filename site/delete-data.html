Delete your Flicky Cricket data

Last updated: 10 November 2026

How to ask for your data to be deleted

  1. Email support@flickycricket.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us which device you play on (iPhone, iPad or Android) and, if you can, your player name. If you bought anything, include the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt.
  3. We will reply to confirm, and complete the deletion within 30 days.

You do not need an account to play Flicky Cricket, so there is nothing to log in to or close.

What is deleted

  • Analytics data linked to your device (game events such as matches played).
  • Any purchase records held by us and our payment partner RevenueCat that are not needed for tax law.
  • Anything you have sent us by email, once your request is complete.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Records of purchases that the law requires us to keep for tax (up to 6 years in the UK).
  • Your game progress is stored on your device, not by us. Deleting the game from your device removes it.

Advertising data

Ads in the game are served by Google AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

More detail is in our privacy policy.
