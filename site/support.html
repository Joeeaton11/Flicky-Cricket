Support

Stuck, found a bug, or lost a purchase? We're here to help.

Email support@flickycricket.com

Please include your device (e.g. iPhone 15, Pixel 8) and what happened. We usually reply within 2 working days.

Common questions

I bought something but didn't get it

Close and reopen the game, then go to Shop → Restore Purchases. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with the date and your App Store or Google Play receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: Google Play → Order history → Request a refund.

How do I remove ads?

Buy "No Ads" in the Shop. It removes the ads between matches. Optional reward videos (for bonus coins) stay available if you want them.

I got a new phone. Can I keep my progress?

Purchases come back with Restore Purchases. Game progress is saved on your device, so install the game on the new phone and restore purchases from the Shop.

How do I stop personalised ads?

iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Android: Settings → Google → Ads. You'll still see ads, but they won't be personalised. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the game removes your saved progress. To ask us to delete analytics or purchase data, email support@flickycricket.com.
